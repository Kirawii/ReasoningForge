# Visual contract

- **Artifact:** ReasoningForge baseline and Kimi-OPMD result figures.
- **Target format:** paper-ready full-width figures plus a Markdown/CSV result table.
- **Core claim:** Kimi-OPMD with `tau=0.03`, two inner updates, and 200 outer iterations remains competitive with GRPO while maintaining a material but non-locking mirror force.
- **Reviewer question:** Does the method improve or preserve task reward, and does the mirror term actually regulate policy movement without numerical collapse?
- **Evidence layers:** main result, mechanism, hyperparameter diagnostic, limitation.
- **Source data:** copied AutoDL `metrics.jsonl` and `run_config.json` files under `analysis/experiments/`.
- **Statistics / uncertainty:** one seed only; no confidence intervals. Tau-sweep ranges are within-run IQRs, not cross-seed uncertainty.
- **Figure map:** Figure 1 learning curves; Figure 2 Kimi mechanism dynamics; Figure 3 tau diagnostic; Figure 4 compute-aware comparison.
- **Caption role:** state the experimental setting, visible takeaway, and single-seed limitation.
- **Output formats:** SVG and PDF for editable/vector use; 300-dpi PNG for review.
- **Traceability:** each figure has a source-data CSV generated by `analysis/plot_results.py`.
